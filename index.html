M.S. Student, Metaverse and Culture Contents
Soongsil University

I'm an M.S. student advised by Jungjin Lee at the Enhanced Experience Lab. I study how people enjoy, create, learn, and connect in virtual worlds — from creators building VTuber personas to audiences sharing emotions at virtual concerts. Before research, I founded and directed the virtual idol group Verse'day, which shaped my interest in the people behind and in front of virtual entertainment.
